Our Story

A family kitchen that grew into South Carolina's go-to caterer for authentic Indian celebrations.

Since day one

Rooted in home-style Gujarati cooking

Khandelwal Caterers & Events started with recipes passed down through generations — the same Undhiyo, Dal Dhokli, and Farsan you'd find at a family gathering back home. Today we cater weddings, corporate events, and festivals across Orangeburg and South Carolina, while keeping every dish rooted in that home-style flavor.

FAQ

Your questions, answered

Do you cater weddings and large receptions?

Yes — from an intimate puja to a 500-guest wedding, we handle full-service buffets, live stations, and plated dinners.

Can the menu be customized?

Absolutely. Menus are built around your guest count, event type, budget, and dietary needs — including a full farali (fasting) menu on request.

Do you offer vegetarian and Jain options?

Yes — full vegetarian and Jain-friendly menus are available alongside our non-veg offerings.

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend 4–6 weeks for weddings and large events, though we can often accommodate shorter notice for smaller gatherings — just ask.
